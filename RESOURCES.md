# Game AI Engineering Resources

## Knowledge

- [Book chapter: "Game Loop", *Game Programming Patterns*, Robert Nystrom](https://gameprogrammingpatterns.com/game-loop.html)
  Free online. "One small step, one giant step" explains scaling movement by elapsed time so it runs at the same real speed on any machine. Use for: delta time, update loops.
- [Article: "Fix Your Timestep!", Glenn Fiedler (Gaffer On Games)](https://gafferongames.com/post/fix_your_timestep/)
  The classic on variable vs fixed timesteps, and why simulation behavior can depend on the dt you pass. Use for: dt pitfalls, when to move to a fixed step.
